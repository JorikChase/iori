Clip lab: a mock-up of the editor's clip gestures with two lanes of clips from the library's latest takes. Drag a clip's top or bottom edge to trim it (a warped clip stretches instead); hold the edge first, then drag inward, for a fade. Drag the seam between touching clips to roll the cut; hold it first for a crossfade. Hold a clip for cut, copy, move and delete. Twist a clip with two fingers past a quarter turn to reverse it. Tap a clip to select it: the corner dot shows its warp, warp mode, original tempo, transpose, gain and fade curve. Two-finger tap undoes; pinch zooms; the bottom dot plays.
