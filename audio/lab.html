Wheel lab: an A/B test of the iOS-style wheel picker against the app's current gestures. List tasks (pick a synth setup, a recent take, a time signature): hold-and-release fan of buds versus the wheel opened by a tap or by press-drag-release. Value tasks (tempo 20 to 300 BPM in 0.1 steps, A4 reference 415 to 466 Hz): the slide gesture, where sideways distance sets the gear, versus digit wheels. Each trial shows a target; time to the correct commit, wrong commits and overshoots are recorded in this browser and can be copied as JSON from the results screen.
