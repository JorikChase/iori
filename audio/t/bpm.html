Touch prototype of the tempo view: a whole song drawn as a vertical bar strip, drag to select a part and its tempo is detected and becomes the shared tempo, tempo changes shown as a step trace. Top-right opens a file, the record dot records from the microphone (both are kept in the library), hold the bottom-right dot still to spin a wheel of every take and release on one, tap it to select the whole song. Tap the detected tempo to set the shared tempo exactly on a digit wheel.
