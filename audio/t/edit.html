Multitrack editor prototype: vertical tracks with time running downward. Tap the bottom dot to play or stop; tap empty space to place the playhead; drag empty space to scroll; pinch to zoom. Hold a clip for cut, copy, move, reverse and delete; hold a track head for mute, solo, arm and remove. The top-left dot adds a track. The bottom-right dot opens snap, push and the take shelf: the four latest recordings and files, and a file bud that opens any audio file and places it at the playhead. Takes sent from the other tools land at the playhead. Two-finger tap undoes.
