Multitrack editor prototype: vertical tracks with time running downward. Tap the bottom dot to play or stop; tap empty space to place the playhead; drag empty space to scroll; pinch to zoom. Drag a clip's top or bottom edge to trim it (a warped clip stretches instead; with snap on the edge snaps to half beats and to other clips); hold the edge first, then drag inward, for a fade. Drag the seam between touching clips to roll the cut; hold it first for a crossfade. Hold a clip for cut, copy, move and delete; twist it with two fingers past a quarter turn to reverse it. Tap a clip to select it: the bottom-right dot then shows its warp, warp mode, original tempo, transpose, gain and fade curve; slide a readout to change it, or tap it for a wheel that sets the exact value. Hold a track head for mute, solo, arm and remove. The top-left dot adds a track. With no clip selected the bottom-right dot opens snap, push, the take shelf (a wheel of every take in the library, newest first, each row its kind and length, under an open-file row that opens any audio file; the chosen take is placed at the playhead) and save: it mixes the audible clips down into a new library take, then turns green for a few seconds, and a second tap shares the mix as a WAV file. Takes sent from the other tools land at the playhead. Two-finger tap undoes.
