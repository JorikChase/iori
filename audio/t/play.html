Practice player prototype: a vertical waveform scrolls up past a fixed playhead. Tap the bottom dot to play or pause; drag it sideways to change speed and up or down to change pitch. Drag the waveform to scrub, tap it to jump, hold then drag on it to mark a loop, tap the loop to clear it. Top-right picks the source: each tap steps through the three most recent takes in the library, then opens a file; bottom-right toggles the loop trainer that speeds up each pass.
