Recorder: tap the centre dot to record from the microphone and again to stop; while recording, tap anywhere else to drop a marker. With retro capture on (bottom-right), the dotted ring fills with the last two minutes heard; hold the dot and drag down to save that many seconds as a take. The top-right dot opens an audio file from the device as a take. Takes stack below: tap to play, swipe left to delete, swipe right to export as WAV, hold for place on the editor timeline, export and delete. If the microphone is refused the centre dot shows a crossed-out microphone; tap it to ask again.
