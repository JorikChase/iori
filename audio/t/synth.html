Touch prototype of the finished synth seed: double-tap the seed to mute it; hold the top-right dot and drag away to rotate between Serum-style, FM and mono setups; hold a coloured dot on the left edge for that category's genes; the garden is kept between visits. Also: drag out of the centre to grow a mutating branch, rest to drop an anchor, release on an anchor to park it. Tap parked branches to switch each one on or off so several play together with the seed, drag one to re-mutate it or back onto the seed to remove it. Hold the centre still for the path menu (a ring fills around it first; a drag at any moment before that grows a branch instead), the top-left dot for distortion and filter layers (now part of each sound and mutated by branches), the bottom-right dot for the gene spiral. Everything the synth plays is kept for four minutes: tap the dot on the right edge to place the last 30 seconds on the editor timeline, or hold it and drag down along the edge to choose from 10 seconds up to four minutes, then release to place it; release back at the start to cancel. In the gene spiral and in the menus, hold still on a number and lift without sliding to open a wheel for its exact value. Two-finger tap undoes.
