Quick recorder prototype: tap the centre dot to record or stop; while recording, tap anywhere else to drop a marker. With retro capture on, the dotted ring fills with the last two minutes heard; hold the dot and drag down to save that many seconds as a take. Takes stack below: tap to play, swipe left to delete, swipe right to export as WAV. Top-right switches microphone and test signal, bottom-right toggles retro capture.
