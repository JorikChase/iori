## How to fly it
1. Open the engine with the flag:
   ```
   http://localhost:8781/iris-engine/?ui=31&flydown=1
   ```
   Seeds exist for eyes 26, 09 and 35, and `?eye=NN` picks one. A kept session wins over `?eye=`; File ▸ New clears it.
2. CAM FREE. Hold the right button on the iris (on touch, a still finger). The optics step ×2 → ×16. Keep holding
   about 0.8 s at ×16 and the camera lands on the surface at that point.
   - It descends from the matching height and tilts from straight down to 24° below the horizon over about 2 s.
3. Walk controls:
   - WASD walks; ←/→ turn.
   - A left drag looks around.
   - The wheel, or Q/E, change the height (4 µm to 2 mm).
   - Esc, or **Leave** in the Camera window, takes you back.
4. **Fly down** in the Camera window lands at the view centre from any zoom.
5. **Detail** toggles the cascade, for comparison with today's engine.
6. The light group ("Light round the eye") works in the walk too. Sun at 7 h gives grazing light across the crypts.

## How it is built (`flydown.js`)

**Inputs, per eye.** `data/<ref>.seed.png`, `.steer.png`, `.steer2.png` and `.seed.json`, built by
`study/flydown/seedtex.py`.
- They store the photo's finest-band residual plus the steering fields, in the tissue's own uv.
- The mapping is the engine's coordinate map at the fit pose, exported from the page as
  `study/shots/map-<ref>-WxH.f32.png`.
- About 6.5 MB per eye, 20 MB for the three.

**Generator.** One fragment pass per 128² page; a pointwise port of `rig.alg_proposal`.
- The page content is a pure function of tissue position, so pages can be dropped and regenerated at will.
- The by-example share of D′ is not ported. It needs the parent page; see the open items.

**Page cache.**
- **Coordinates:** the tissue's Cartesian plane, r = 2 + 4 v mm (as `ablTexture`).
- **Levels:** five, with texels from 4 µm (ℓ0) to 0.25 µm (ℓ4).
- **Atlas:** an RG16F atlas of 4096², 1024 slots, 64 MB. The channels hold D and a per-cell birth jitter.
- **Page table:** an RG32F table of 512 × 843 covering every level whole. Each entry holds the slot + 1 and the birth time.
  The 1 µm floor bounds the depth, so no clipmap is needed.
- **Eviction:** LRU among pages the view did not ask for.

**Requests.**
- A 64 × 48 readback of the same camera through the variant's debug 17, which returns uv and footprint per sample.
- Two levels per sample.
- One texel per **CSS** pixel. At device pixels a ×4 view needed about 900 pages; at CSS pixels it needs 250.

**Growth.**
- Order: the anisotropic distance from the view centre (along the strand ×1, across ×3.5), coarse levels first.
- A page fades in over 0.9 s, each 12 µm cell with up to 0.6 s of extra delay.
- While the queue drains, the coarser level shows through.

**The variant.** String replacement on the tissue variant's source; `tissue.js` only exposes it via
`T.photoSource()`.
- **Samplers:** both are reused from K1's origin textures. The program sits at WebKit's 16-sampler limit, and K1 is off
  while the layer model owns the atlas. The variant throws if a K1 read survives.
- **Albedo:** `tsA.rgb *= exp(D)`.
- **Replacing the statistics:** the statistics' 6 µm octave and half of the 12 µm one give way as the cascade comes in
  (decision 2). The 24 and 48 µm octaves stay.
- **Micro-relief:** a micro normal from ∇D (brighter = raised, 1.5 µm per unit of log luminance).
- **Walk camera:** `u_walk` calls `shadeInterior` from inside the chamber, so there is no cornea in the way.
  - The footprint is the pixel angle × the distance.
  - The march takes 96 steps, and never starts behind the camera.
  - The sky is the room, dimmed.

**When it is used.** Only while the footprint is below 5.9 µm per CSS px, or during the walk.
- At every coarser view the engine draws with the tissue program itself, so the far view is the same program and the
  same bits.
- Without `?flydown=1` nothing loads.

**Debug modes in the variant:**
- 16: the hit point.
- 17: uv and footprint.
- 18: the multiplier and the replacement weight.
- 19: the detail alone, ×4.
- 20: page presence.

## Numbers
Measured on the real GPU: the Browser pane (WebKit) on this Mac, the canvas at 2524 × 1960 device px, with
`EXT_disjoint_timer_query`, median of 6.

| | GPU per frame |
|---|---|
| far view (tissue program) | 83 ms |
| ×16 crop, detail off | 196 ms |
| ×16 crop, detail on | 224 ms (+14 %) |
| walk, 45 µm up, 96 march steps | 264 ms |
| one 128² page generated | ≈ 1.0 ms (8 pages: 7.9–8.1 ms) |

- **Pages per view:** 249 at ×4 (3 µm per CSS px). At ×16, 297 at once. In the walk, about 630.
  The 1024-slot cache filled during one fly-down and evicted correctly.
- **Time to sharp:** at the default budget of 8 pages per frame, about 32 frames for a ×4 view (≈ 0.25 s of GPU
  generation).
- **Comparison with the brief:** the brief's 32 pages at 3 ms per frame (§5.1) assumed about 0.1 ms per page. Ours is
  10× that. The first octave's seed demodulation is 225 fetches per texel; see the open items.
- **Frame times:** these are for a 4.9 Mpx canvas in a hidden pane. In a visible window, and per CSS pixel, they are
  smaller. Re-measure there before tuning.

## Open, in rough priority
1. **Seed demodulation.** Precompute the seed's phase per lattice cell into the seed texture, which would make
   generation about 10× cheaper. Then raise the budget, or keep it and lower the per-frame cost.
2. **By-example share of D′.** Generate ℓ+1 from the resident ℓ page (patch search ±4 in the parent). That is the
   hierarchical version of `rig.upscale_self`; ancestors then have to stay pinned.
3. **Walk relief.** It is the 6 µm-texel tissue heightfield, so crypt walls are smooth ramps with streaks at grazing
   angles. A micro-height layer (decision 3) should add D's relief to the march, not only to the normal. Cast shadows
   from the sun are weak at walk scale, because the self-shadow march is short.
4. **Optics → walk hand-off.** It lands where you held, but the framing changes: the orbit camera's eye rotation is
   not carried into the walk's straight-down start. Leaving returns to the full view rather than easing out through
   the optics steps.
5. **Persistence (D6), giant render (D7).** Not started.
6. **×16 windowed re-bake blocks** in today's engine (`T.refocus`). The prototype does not use that path, so they are
   still there.
7. **Seed files.** `data/*.seed*` add 20 MB to the repo. Before deploying, exclude them, or move them to the eye file
   as a section (D2).
8. **The seed map.** It was exported with `zoomPhoto` about 0.3 % off the case's (smoothing had not settled). That
   puts it up to about 3 native px off at the limbus. Re-export with the case zoom before measuring correlation.

## Rig
`await import('/iris-engine/tools/flydown/rig.js')` gives `ready`, `crop(m, cx, cy)`, `tick(n)` and
`cap(name, view, { ticks })`.
- A hidden pane runs no rAF, so `tick` drives `flydown.js`.
- `cap` settles the growth and POSTs to `study/shots/fd-<name>.png`.
- Pictures: `study/flydown/proto-x16-off-on.jpg` and `proto-walk-noon-dawn.jpg`.
