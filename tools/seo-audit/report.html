§1Verdict

The technical side has essentially finished its job. Every critical finding from September is closed: the leaks, the canonicals, the compression, the 63 dead-end pages, the duplicate identity. Search positions roughly halved on both domains, more iori.me pages are being indexed than ever, and the cross-domain duplicates are draining out of the index as intended.

What did not happen is people. Clicks went from one to four in a month. Direct traffic fell by a third. The sites are now technically correct and still almost nobody is searching for them, which is not a problem any further SEO work will solve.

The brand query has not moved

"3die" went from 19 impressions at position 5.4 to 16 at position 6.2, still no clicks. That is within noise. "iori" still returns nothing at all on iori.me.

The whole of search across both domains in the last month is ten distinct queries: eight on iori.me, two on 3die.fr. Everything indexable is indexed and ranking far better than it was. The ceiling now is demand, not technique.

§7Settled — do not reopen

Three recommendations from the September audit were considered and rejected, with reasons that still hold:

  • no
    Do not add text or headings to the experiment pages.

    This was the first audit's headline recommendation. The menu supplied the internal links instead, which is what actually mattered, and the pages are full-screen canvases whose viewport must not be disturbed.

  • no
    Do not hide an H1 for crawlers.

    Text hidden purely for search engines is the one thing Google treats as deceptive, and an H1 is a minor signal beside the title tag, which every page already has, unique and accurate.

  • no
    Do not speed up the iori.me landing.

    It grows from the first cell by design and reads well on a phone. Crawlers never wait for it: the generated navigation block ships 83 real links in the raw HTML.

The measured outcome supports all three. Internal links were the lever, not body text; the pages that entered the index this month did so on links alone.
